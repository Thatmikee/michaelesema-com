A name people can trust before they taste anything.

Create an identity for Sarah Bellus, a Nigerian caterer in the UK who cooks to order, and carry it from the logo to the printed menu and a one-page website.

She needed a name people could trust before they tasted anything, and a mark that still read on a phone screen and on a sticker the size of a coin.

A serving spoon stands in for the dot of the i. The palette is aubergine and cream with a single orange, taken from the colour of jollof.

One mark that had to work on a letterhead and on a mug.

I joined B’s Hive in Abuja as Head Accountant in 2019 and ended up responsible for how the hotel looked as well as how it balanced. I designed the logo, a B and an H locked into one block, and the letterhead that carried it, with the monogram repeated as a watermark.

The same mark went onto notebooks, jotters, pens, mugs and flasks, alongside promotions, social posts, event flyers and digital signage.

For printed pieces I wrote the briefs for outside print vendors, checked proofs and delivered final files to budget and deadline.
